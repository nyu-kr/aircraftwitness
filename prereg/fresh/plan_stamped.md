# Fresh-place replication: preregistered protocol

Written2026-11-05 before fresh-study audio. Based on `b5/PREREG.draft.md`, with the coordinator's11:17 detector-selection and11:26 population-clock revisions. The metadata-eligible pool was certified before collection. These exact rules and pool commitment must be committed and externally timestamped; the remaining execution gates below must also pass before any fetch. Registration alone does not certify unimplemented acquisition or scoring code. The frozen paper, dataset and release are not edited.

## Cover note

- Aim: 24 physical places new to every entrant, minimum 20, one sensor per place, at most six clear-pass clips per place and at most one source recording per local place-day. Each clip is 300 s. Depth can be below six; a five-day deployment cannot supply six independent place-days.
- Estimand: location success **conditional on the frozen audio detector finding a clear pass**, not the unconditional random-clip rate of the abandoned b5 proposal. Screening attrition is reported separately. No unconditional-rate or new-place claim is inferred from existing snapshot pass counts.
- Pipeline: as each source recording lands on NYU it is validated, detected and, if admitted by the fixed rule, immediately released as an opaque packet to the frozen locators. No wait for whole-source or whole-set acquisition. Wrong-audio work waits only for its assigned donor. Source servers run concurrently, with at most four simultaneous requests per public server across all workers. Use available authorized allocations under the shared cluster CPU/memory ceilings, never an invented second user/account.
- Proposed deadlines, conditional on a proven eligible pool and registered bytes by 11:50 EST: first primary packets 12:05, screening/acquisition ends 14:30, primary ends 15:30, controls end 16:45, verifier's independent reconciliation/scoring ends 17:30. These are targets, not measured transfer-time guarantees. Results not independently reconciled by 17:30 are deferred to version 2, never inserted as a completed study today.
- Historical cost at60s slack: scaling to six clips/place gives0.87to1.28allocatedCPU-hours/place for three entrants/three arms. This is not a300s-slack measurement. Intake, screening and new-day indexing are additional. Hard600s invocation caps imply at most36CPU-hours/place, not a consumption forecast. Prior b4 peak MaxRSS2,020.4MiB over891rows supports4GiB there; the runner reports older-rerun3.36GB, so fresh uniformly requests6GiB pending staged memory readback. See `COST_RECEIPT.txt` and `DESIGN_RECEIPT.txt`.

## Frozen rules

1. **Roles and separation.** Geo-verify2 alone holds physical-place identities, published positions, used-place union, device clock receipts and scoring truth. It also constructs the private candidate schedule or delegates private acquisition only to a process it controls. The runner sees opaque packets, audio, boxes, claimed UTC and flights, but never source names, source IDs, original filenames, URLs, true positions or private groupings. Shared reports contain aggregates only. E2E's verifier is not the fresh truth holder. No site is admitted, rejected or replaced using a locator answer, score or aircraft-derived clock fit.

2. **Pool and novelty.** Eclipse Soundscapes in CONUS is primary. Geo-verify2 deduplicates physical places and selects one sensor/place. Used means audio ever input to any locator version, choosing/tuning the detector or locator settings, or an earlier test/development set. Planned task lists, metadata census and immutable audibility labelling without locator/tuning do not themselves constitute use. Earlier audibility work fetched pass windows at candidate places but did not run a locator or tune this selector. The pre-audibility selection chronology is recorded in `verify/findings.md:808` (b3 used selector SHA256 f41ea9972888 via its pull caller), `geoloc/data/a2o_b3/PREREG.md:20` (all original b3 arms complete07:59 on11-04) and audibility commit `559dfb2` (rule frozen18:51:43 on11-04). `verify/findings.md:561` also records a05:40 operation-derived composite hash including that detector. The current exact selector bytes were independently hashed on NYU, not inferred from mtime. Exclude every used place and require published position >5km by haversine from every used or other admitted place. Record which prior rosters and actual exposure receipts were checked; an incomplete union does not certify novelty. Target24, minimum20. Concurrent e2e keeps its unbiased frozen sample: the two verifiers privately reconcile it against fresh before either exposes overlapping audio to entrants, dropping fresh overlaps within5km or registering a specific fresh-first ordering before exposure. A durable receipt, not a message alone, releases that gate. Locus is fallback only under the same novelty/position rules and a documented source-delay allowance within the300s budget; no ad hoc source is added after audio begins.

3. **Published position gate.** The actual fixed microphone position must be public, non-obscured and supported as a recorder location, not merely a geocoded town centre. Device/deployment ambiguity, mobile mounts and pins with insufficient position evidence fail before audio. Geo-verify2 retains the publisher's position and uncertainty unrefined, with evidence privately. A coordinate's printed decimals are not measured accuracy. Do not de-obscure or geocode a more precise position. Accepted evidence is the collector-published fixed-recorder pin, checked against the primary source and left unrefined; its measurement uncertainty is unreported. Scoring is agreement with that published pin, not a claim of surveyed metre-level truth.

4. **Population clock model and300s slack, revised11:26 before audio.** The coordinator approved this operational model for chime-labelled deployments:95s initial-setting allowance +5s/day since the deployment's first recording +1s file-start allowance, total<=300s; additionally require clip end no later than40days after that first recording. Use the complete deployment's earliest known recording, not the first conveniently downloaded file. Reject resets or unresolvable deployment boundaries.95s rounds up the largest resolved initial offset in the old used-site fleet,94.6s.20ppm is1.728s/day;5s/day is an approximately2.9-fold planning allowance, not an exact threefold specification guarantee. **This is an empirical population assumption, not an independently measured per-recorder UTC bound.** Setting before the first recording, device outliers and unmeasured acquisition conditions can violate it; report this residual risk and never label these clips certified true-clock ground truth. The old fleet also has initial offsets73.4and85.1s, so the single1.31s/day fit does not validate2s setting. The superseded2+5d+1<=60 rule never ran. No new-place aircraft alignment or locator result admits a deployment. Locus needs a separately documented complete source-delay allowance within300s and cannot pass on capture NTP alone. **Every entrant uses300s start slack.** The comparison is b3's reported20km/300s robustness condition, not its60s primary; the wider-clock experiment is disclosed as a design change.

5. **Day and flight gate, before audio.** Consider every recording day in the frozen metadata with an available cached adsb.lol day and both P/W flight windows. All clip samples must lie in local 06:00 <= time <19:00. Time zone comes from the scorer's private published-position lookup, with DST applied. For a place-day retain the inherited b3 `n4>0` condition: at least one published aircraft sample in daytime with `hypot(horizontal_distance, barometric_altitude_AMSL_in_metres) <4000 m`, using `sources/a2o_b3/bin/cov.py`'s horizontal formula. This is a geometric traffic gate, not proof of audibility, and is not a new conjunction with the separate `altitude<6000 ft within10km` diagnostic. Report geometric day counts and missing-day/receiver coverage separately. The final must identify the private flight cache and applied gate bytes. No clock eligibility is inferred from this gate.

6. **Candidate/day/recording order.** Before any audio, geo-verify2 freezes a private seed 20261105 and shuffled order over sorted eligible physical identities, sensor identities, eligible local days and recordings; each level uses a separate documented seed namespace. Draw one sensor uniformly from eligible sensors at a place before drawing recordings. Only members with documented duration >=300 s and eligible clock/day spans can be attempted. Try up to four source recordings per place-day in the frozen order. For a long member, inspect at most its first six non-overlapping eligible 300 s windows, ordered by start; do not move a window to an acoustic or ADS-B peak. No concatenation across gaps. The first window passing rule 7 becomes that place-day's only selected clip. Stop that day's screening immediately. Each place contributes its first at most six qualifying days in frozen order. Screen at most 40 metadata-qualified places in frozen order, round-robin by attempted day, taking the first 24 that obtain at least one qualifying day. A deterministic in-order consumer resolves out-of-order fetch completion; fastest download is not the selection rule. A place never drops out after admission on a locator result.

7. **Clear-pass gate.** Run the unchanged b3 pull detector `bench/bin/detect.py`, SHA256 `f41ea9972888a671eada52fab58e019c50462d7e3e8928c82bd645c7eb8d43b7` as read on NYU. Keep iff `any(p['clear'] for p in detect(audio)['passes'])`. Its existing thresholds include drop>=0.04, monotonic fraction>=0.8, median contrast>=8 and >=24 half-second frames, with existing track/segment gates unchanged. Detection is audio-only and does not use truth, flight identity or the locator. No listening override, threshold tuning or PANNs/band-score substitute. Decode/length/silence failures are screening failures with reasons, not fabricated clear clips. Detection logs contain acquisition, selection reason and byte/hash receipts privately. Report attempted places/days/recordings/windows, qualifying days and excluded/error counts. This gate favours a detector used in developing the reference, so public-tool results, not reference alone, answer replication.

8. **Acquisition and pipeline.** All fetching, extraction, decoding, detection and locator computation occur on NYU, never the home link. Eclipse members are read individually by archive central-directory/member HTTP ranges, not full daily ZIPs. Existing reader `audible/bin/rzip.py`, NYU SHA256 `97c7e601d665fbeed5df62db5f813e9cc7c2441c629633fb06a6c142d7bbcde8`, is the starting implementation, not authority to ignore its verified byte limits or failures. Deflated members may require their entire compressed stream; record actual compressed bytes and decode cost. Shared per-server cap four across fresh and e2e, using the common `prepare.HTTP` limiter with persistent slot/state anchors at `/scratch/NETID/geo/study_fetch_locks` (NETID is a privacy placeholder, not a second root). All workers use the same actual configured path and destination-host alias map, including redirects, audio, history and terrain. Never unlink flock anchors. Exponential backoff60s doubling to15min, stop after three consecutive backoffs, honor429 and published start-rate limits. Pin and exercise the shared limiter before the first source request. Snapshot public access is not a fresh grant of rights: each original's applicable audio licence is checked privately. No accounts/credentials appear in commands or reports, only credential file paths. Selection precedes release. Each packet-ready handoff is an atomically installed durable file with packet, input hashes and work items that consumers re-read; a one-shot message alone is not completion.

9. **Packets and identical inputs.** Every selected clip becomes an opaque random packet. Strip source-identifying metadata and original paths. The verifier draws one independent uniform20km square per packet containing its published place by the inherited boxes rule. Claimed UTC is the chime-labelled file start plus clip offset under the declared population clock model, with no added random offset. All entrants receive bit-identical300s audio, that square, t0 and300s tolerance. Flights: same adsb.lol day bytes, square+0.3deg, t0-20min through clip end+21min; W rebuilt at shifted time. Reference gets GLO-90 DEM with inherited padding; dopplerprint its standard CONUS GLO-30 handling. Unheard indexes exactly the permitted days/flight availability, with any different spatial ingest explicitly recorded. All input, flight-source and runtime commitments are frozen before first packet; compare hashes at consumer read.

11. **Arms and consensus.** P: real clip, place box and time. W: same audio/box with t0+7200s and that shifted flight window. A: wrong audio from another place with a disjoint box; freeze the b3 seeded pairing rule, seed20261105, before audio. Each place's selected clips share a preassigned donor place chosen without outcomes; choose donor clip by corresponding qualifying-day rank, cycling deterministically where depths differ. A waits for its donor and never silently falls back to the recipient. If a full one-to-one disjoint pairing is impossible, the inherited b4 seeded disjoint-donor replacement rule applies and donor reuse is counted. Final donor map is committed before first A task. Consensus: all three answers pairwise within1km -> mean, else closest pair within1km -> mean, else abstain. It is descriptive, not another confirmatory test.

12. **Compute and order.** One Slurm allocation per arriving packet, at most one entrant invocation at once per allocation, same4CPUs and6GiB for every invocation, numerical threads1, same hard600s invocation wall budget. Run all three P invocations first, then interleave W/A when dependencies arrive. A pack/array allocation can hold sequential invocations but never erase their individual timeout/exit/row receipts. Shared running demand <=2,800CPUs; use available authorized allocations under the actual user's QOS, cpu_short only where its queue limits allow. Respect the shared32CPU cpu_short cap, not per-source copies. Record MaxRSS; an OOM gets one16GiB retry, independent of answer, with both attempts counted and reported. No answer-based rerun. A setup failure is fixed only before study invocation or reported as a deviation, not tuned using the answer. Job caps and a pipeline timeline are operational, not completion evidence.

13. **Freeze, stop and attrition.** Screening stops14:30EST regardless of acoustic or locator success beyond the registered gate. The selected manifest and private place grouping are sealed and hashed then. At least20 admitted new places are required; fewer means a documented feasibility shortfall, not a20-place replication. All packets admitted before the stop remain in the P denominator, including missing/crashed/timed-out/no-answer rows and code mismatches, which are misses. All P invocations stop15:30, controls16:45. No scores are revealed until those stops and the manifest is final. No early success/futility look and no extra candidates after seeing results. Report W/A on every admitted packet with missing rows as misses; also report their longest complete opaque-order prefix with P beside it, explicitly secondary.

14. **Scoring and chance, inherited b3 rule.** Geo-verify2 scores haversine distance, Earth radius6371km, hit<=1km from published truth. Physical place is the unit. **Primary chance is b3's empirical cross-place shift null**, preserving answers' offsets within their own boxes and comparing with other places' truth offsets; place-specific P(at least one hit) feeds the one-sided Poisson-binomial tail. The uniform-box calculation is reported beside it, not silently substituted for the registered shift null. The scorer must resolve place weighting, unequal depths, missing rows and box-edge area exactly by the inherited scorer bytes before freezing, with injected wrong-place/deleted-input/missing-row tests. Reference and consensus are descriptive. Each public entrant is a separate replication question at p<0.05; only if both public entrants pass may the joint claim say both replicated. Any claim that at least one replicated needs a predeclared multiplicity correction, not an unadjusted minimum p.

15. **Planning power, not a claim of yield.** With24places and6clips each, the conservative full-disk uniform bound pi/400 per clip gives1.109 expected hit-places and requires4 observed hit-places for a one-sided tail0.0231. At20places it gives0.924 expected and4hit-places tail0.0122. These are uniform-null planning scenarios, not the actual shift-null threshold. At assumed marginal true place-hit probability0.25, powers are88.5% and77.5%, respectively. That probability is an assumption, not estimated from detector-selected old successes or broad snapshot pass counts. Variable1-6clip depths require their actual registered chance probabilities. `DESIGN_RECEIPT.txt` was calculated on NYU before study audio.

16. **Reports and independent readback.** Per entrant: all packets and places, hits, places with a hit, empirical/uniform chance and p, answered/abstention/failure reasons, precision with Wilson95% explicitly ignoring clustering, largest-place hit share and primary p without it, W/A controls and coverage, input/build mismatches, OOM/retries and missing rows. Also report screening attrition and actual clock evidence by class. An independently re-read final manifest, raw result rows, input/code hashes and scorer output must reconcile counts/fields, not merely an ok:true receipt. Print one real aggregate value from the scorer before any completion claim. Shared files: aggregate `fresh/results.json`, `fresh/SUMMARY.md`; private truth and linking keys stay with geo-verify2. JSON contract: top-level `places`, `clips`, `slack_s:300`, `entrants`; entrant keys `reference`, `dopplerprint`, `unheard`, `consensus`; each has `P`, `W`, `A`; every arm has `hit_places`, `places`, `chance_places`, `chance_places_uniform`, `p`, `hits`, `clips`. `chance_places` and `p` are the registered shift-null expectation and place-level Poisson-binomial tail. Emit no placeholder result before the actual scorer runs. No claim about >20 independent places without the scorer's physical-place census.

The coordinator granted standing go11:32 conditional on at least20 metadata-eligible places; the46-place certificate satisfies that metadata condition, not unfinished execution proofs.
